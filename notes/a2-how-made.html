How this was made

AI Energy Use: Research Briefing

For CIS 498 assignment A2, I researched the resource costs of AI and built a chart and two-slide business briefing from International Energy Agency (IEA) data.

What I asked AI to do

Using Claude (Opus 5.5) with web search in my CIS 498 Project, I had Claude find and summarize sources from the IEA, EIA, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, and company sustainability reports, and locate a downloadable dataset. My project knowledge included the course syllabus, the COB Framework for AI Use, and the SKEPTICS framework.

Claude then built the chart and two-slide deck from the IEA data file and filled in the factual sections of my assignment documentation.

What I did myself

I downloaded the IEA Energy and AI data annex myself and uploaded it, so the chart was built from the actual numbers instead of from Claude’s summary. I questioned whether the IEA was a reputable source before relying on it, and I wrote my own reflection answers and recommendations.

How I checked it

Claude’s first source list linked mostly to reports instead of downloadable data, and it included a Google paper that wasn’t peer-reviewed, which I replaced with Epoch AI’s dataset. I rejected the first deck’s class-project styling and had it rebuilt as a corporate tracker-style briefing.

I verified every link Claude gave me and checked the dataset myself, creating an account to access it because Claude couldn’t.

Tools

Claude (Opus 5.5), web search, Claude file creation
